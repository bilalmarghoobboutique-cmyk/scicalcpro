💡 How to Use This Calculator

  1. Select your country from the dropdown (currency will auto-change)
  2. Choose metal type (Gold 24K, 22K, 21K, 18K or Silver 999, 925)
  3. Enter the current rate in ANY unit — others will auto-calculate
  4. Enter the weight you want to calculate
  5. Optionally add making charges and tax
  6. Click "Calculate Price" to get the total
  7. Click "🖨️ Print" in header to save/print receipt
