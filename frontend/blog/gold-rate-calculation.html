How Gold Rate is Calculated: Complete Guide to Purity, Weight & Price

📅 08 October 2026 ⏱️ 8 min read 👤 Bilal Marghoob
📢 Advertisement
Responsive — In-Content

Gold is one of the most traded and valued metals in the world. But how is its price actually calculated? Whether you're a jeweller, investor, or just curious, understanding gold rate calculation helps you make better decisions. This guide explains gold purity, weight units, and how daily prices are determined.

1. International Gold Rate

Gold is traded internationally on major exchanges like:

  • LBMA (London Bullion Market Association)
  • COMEX (New York)
  • TOCOM (Tokyo)
  • SGE (Shanghai Gold Exchange)

The international price is quoted in USD per Troy Ounce. One troy ounce = 31.1035 grams.

Example: If international rate is $2,000/oz, then:

  • Price per gram = $2,000 ÷ 31.1035 ≈ $64.30

2. Gold Purity (Karat System)

Pure gold (24K) is too soft for jewelry, so it's mixed with other metals (copper, silver, zinc). The karat number tells you how much gold is in the mix.

Karat Purity % Common Use
24K99.9%Bullion, investment
22K91.6%Jewelry (Pakistan, India)
21K87.5%Jewelry (Middle East)
18K75.0%Western jewelry
14K58.3%Durable jewelry

Formula: Gold content = (Karat ÷ 24) × 100%

3. Weight Units Used for Gold

Different countries use different units:

  • Gram (g) — Global standard
  • Tola — South Asia (Pakistan, India). 1 tola = 11.6638 grams
  • Troy Ounce (oz t) — International. 1 oz t = 31.1035 grams
  • Kilogram (kg) — 1000 grams
  • Masha — 1 masha = 0.972 grams (traditional)
  • Ratti — 1 ratti = 0.1215 grams (traditional)

Conversion table:

  • 1 Tola = 11.6638 grams
  • 1 Tola = 0.375 Troy Ounces
  • 1 Troy Ounce = 2.6667 Tolas
  • 1 Kilogram = 85.735 Tolas

4. The Gold Price Formula

The formula for gold jewelry price:

Total Price = (Weight × Rate) + Making Charges + Tax

Where:

  • Weight — in grams or tola
  • Rate — current rate per gram/tola for that karat
  • Making Charges — jeweller's fee (usually 5-15%)
  • Tax — government tax (varies by country)

5. Step-by-Step Example

Scenario: Buy a 22K gold ring, weight 1 tola, rate Rs 220,000 per tola, making charges 10%, tax 3%.

Step 1 — Metal Price:

  • Weight: 1 tola
  • Rate: Rs 220,000
  • Metal Price = 1 × 220,000 = Rs 220,000

Step 2 — Making Charges (10%):

  • 220,000 × 10% = Rs 22,000

Step 3 — Subtotal:

  • 220,000 + 22,000 = Rs 242,000

Step 4 — Tax (3%):

  • 242,000 × 3% = Rs 7,260

Step 5 — Total Price:

  • 242,000 + 7,260 = Rs 249,260

Total: Rs 249,260 ✅

6. Factors That Affect Gold Price

  • International spot price — Changes every second during trading hours
  • Currency exchange rate — USD to PKR, INR, etc.
  • Import duties — Government taxes on gold import
  • Demand and supply — Wedding season, festivals increase demand
  • Economic conditions — Gold rises during recessions
  • Interest rates — Higher rates = lower gold prices usually
  • Making charges — Varies by jeweller and design
  • Purity — Higher karat = higher price per gram
