Time Calculation: How to Add and Subtract Hours, Minutes, Seconds

📅 08 October 2026 ⏱️ 5 min read 👤 Bilal Marghoob
📢 Advertisement
Responsive — In-Content

Time calculation is tricky because time doesn't use base-10 like regular numbers — it uses base-60 (sexagesimal). When adding 45 minutes + 30 minutes, you don't get 75 minutes — you get 1 hour 15 minutes. This guide teaches you how to add and subtract time correctly, with practical examples.

1. Time Units Refresher

Basic time units:

  • 1 minute = 60 seconds
  • 1 hour = 60 minutes = 3,600 seconds
  • 1 day = 24 hours
  • 1 week = 7 days

2. The Base-60 Rules

The most important rule when calculating time:

⚠️ If seconds ≥ 60, carry over to minutes.
⚠️ If minutes ≥ 60, carry over to hours.

Example: 45 sec + 30 sec = 75 sec = 1 min 15 sec

Example: 45 min + 30 min = 75 min = 1 hour 15 min

3. How to Add Time

Step-by-step method:

  1. Add the seconds first
  2. If seconds ≥ 60, subtract 60 and add 1 to minutes
  3. Add the minutes (including any carry)
  4. If minutes ≥ 60, subtract 60 and add 1 to hours
  5. Add the hours (including any carry)

Example: 2h 45m 30s + 1h 30m 45s

  • Seconds: 30 + 45 = 75s → 75 − 60 = 15s, carry 1 min
  • Minutes: 45 + 30 + 1 = 76 min → 76 − 60 = 16 min, carry 1 hour
  • Hours: 2 + 1 + 1 = 4 hours

Result: 4h 16m 15s ✅

4. How to Subtract Time

Step-by-step method:

  1. Subtract the seconds first
  2. If result is negative, borrow 60 seconds from minutes
  3. Subtract the minutes (with any borrow)
  4. If result is negative, borrow 60 minutes from hours
  5. Subtract the hours (with any borrow)

Example: 5h 15m 30s − 2h 45m 45s

  • Seconds: 30 − 45 = −15s → borrow 1 min: 30 + 60 − 45 = 45s (now minutes reduced by 1)
  • Minutes: 15 − 1 − 45 = −31 min → borrow 1 hour: 15 − 1 + 60 − 45 = 29 min (now hours reduced by 1)
  • Hours: 5 − 1 − 2 = 2 hours

Result: 2h 29m 45s ✅

5. Real Examples

Example 1: Adding

Problem: A movie is 2h 30m long. Another is 1h 45m long. Total time if watched back-to-back?

  • Minutes: 30 + 45 = 75 min → 1h 15m (carry 1 hour)
  • Hours: 2 + 1 + 1 (carry) = 4

Answer: 4 hours 15 minutes

Example 2: Subtracting

Problem: You have 5 hours to complete a task. You've used 3h 25m. How much time is left?

  • Minutes: 0 − 25 → borrow: 60 − 25 = 35 min
  • Hours: 5 − 1 − 3 = 1

Answer: 1 hour 35 minutes

Example 3: Combining

Problem: A factory produces 8h 15m in the morning and 6h 45m in the evening. Total daily production time?

  • Minutes: 15 + 45 = 60 min → 1h (carry)
  • Hours: 8 + 6 + 1 = 15 hours

Answer: 15 hours

6. Real-World Applications

Time calculation is used daily:

  • Project management — Total hours for tasks
  • Payroll — Hours worked by employees
  • Fitness — Total exercise time
  • Travel — Flight durations and layovers
  • Cooking — Multiple dishes ready at once
  • Music production — Track lengths and album totals
