📖 How to Use This Area Calculator

  1. Enter your name (optional)
  2. Select the shape — Rectangle, Circle, Triangle, or Square
  3. Enter the required dimensions
  4. Click "Calculate Area"
  5. View area, perimeter, and formula
  6. Click Print for a receipt

📐 Formulas Used

  • Rectangle: Area = L × W, Perimeter = 2(L+W)
  • Circle: Area = πr², Perimeter = 2πr
  • Triangle: Area = ½ × b × h
  • Square: Area = s², Perimeter = 4s
